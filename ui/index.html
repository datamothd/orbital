Uninstall selected apps?

Selected apps will be removed from your PC. Windows apps are removed for all users and from provisioning for new accounts. Administrator access may be required.

    Enable permanent removal?

    Allows permanent deletion of bundled app files and Windows component payloads after you confirm an uninstall. Orbital cannot undo this; dependent apps or features may need reinstallation or repair. Permission lasts until you turn this off or close Orbital.

    Administrator access required

    Administrator access

    Administrator access is needed to change system-wide settings, inspect apps for all users, remove provisioned apps, and service Windows components. Personal preferences can still be changed without it.

    Relaunching opens the Windows permission prompt and resets unsaved app selections and setup options.
